## 🔗 Delivery Assurance Model

Applying bow-tie methodology to delivery itself. **Hazard:** safety-critical operations governed by software. **Top event:** loss of assurance in the delivered platform — the point at which the system can no longer be trusted to reflect operational reality.

```mermaid

    T1["Standard interpreted<br/>inconsistently"]:::threat --> P1["Requirements traced to<br/>clause and verifier"]:::prev --> TE
    T2["Documented process<br/>differs from field practice"]:::threat --> P2["Operational discovery,<br/>BPMN validated with ops"]:::prev --> TE
    T3["Site demands drive<br/>bespoke builds"]:::threat --> P3["Configurable architecture,<br/>site profiles"]:::prev --> TE
    T4["Calculation cannot<br/>be reproduced"]:::threat --> P4["Deterministic logic,<br/>versioned assumptions"]:::prev --> TE

    TE(("TOP EVENT<br/>Loss of assurance<br/>in the platform")):::top

    TE --> R2["Piloted, staged<br/>site rollout"]:::rec --> C2["Audit findings and<br/>non-conformance"]:::cons
    TE --> R3["Adoption metrics driving<br/>backlog re-prioritisation"]:::rec --> C3["Programme rebuild<br/>or write-off"]:::cons
```
